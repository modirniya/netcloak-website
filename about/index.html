Who we are

NetCloak is published by NeuEra Apps LLC. You can reach us at hello@neuera.app; that address is read by the people who build the app, not by a ticketing system. The terms of use are governed by California law. Our legal documents for every app, with every past version kept readable, live at legal.neuera.app.

Why a free VPN

Because the free ones we tried either asked for an account, hid their limits, or promised things no VPN can deliver. We wanted one that a person could install without giving anything up, that showed its limit as a clock rather than hiding a cap, and that could put its entire data practice in one paragraph. That paragraph is on every page of this site.
